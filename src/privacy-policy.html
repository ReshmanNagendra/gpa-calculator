Privacy Policy

Your data stays yours.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We built this CGPA Calculator to be fully client-side. This means that all calculations, grades, and targets you enter are processed locally on your own device's browser. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any of your academic data or personal information.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

This application does not use cookies for tracking. In the future, we may use your browser's Local Storage to save your course list so you don't have to re-enter it when you return, but this data will never leave your device.

3. Third-Party Services

We use third-party CDNs (like Google Fonts and html2canvas) to load fonts and scripts. These providers may receive your IP address when serving these files, governed by their respective privacy policies.
